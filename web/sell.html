Prepare inventory for DealzTT review.

Vehicles, machinery, repossessions, and liquidation inventory are the first priority. The current public site does not yet accept, publish, schedule, or settle consignments online.

Prepare a consignment pack

Have the item title, location, ownership or transfer information, condition details, known defects, opening-price guidance, fulfillment method, and clear images ready for review. Published auctions have no reserve.

Vehicle and machinery records

Include make, model, year, mileage or hours, operating condition, keys, damage, documentation status, and any available inspection evidence. Do not publish private document numbers.

What happens next

Consignment review, approval, fee terms, scheduling, buyer payment, pickup, and seller settlement are not connected to the public site yet.
