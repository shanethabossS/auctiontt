How selling works

List your item

Sign in with SOV ID, submit your ID there once, then list the item below with a title, category, condition and known defects, ownership/transfer status, a starting price and clear photos.

Pay the listing fee

Pay TT$40 by bank transfer using your reference. When we confirm it, your auction goes live for 7 days. Vehicles and machinery may be asked for extra condition and document detail, and DealzTT can take down anything that breaks the rules.

Auction runs & you get paid

Your lot runs as a no-reserve timed auction with a two-minute soft close. The buyer pays within 48 hours, and you're paid out after pickup or delivery is confirmed.
