How selling works

Submit your inventory

Send us the item(s) with title, location, condition, known defects, ownership/transfer status, opening-price guidance, and clear photos. Start a consignment enquiry below — no payment or ID documents needed at this stage.

We review & schedule

DealzTT reviews the consignment, confirms fee terms, and schedules the auction. Vehicles and machinery may need extra condition and document detail before going live.

Auction runs & you get paid

Your lot runs as a no-reserve timed auction with a two-minute soft close. The buyer pays within 48 hours, and you're paid out after pickup or delivery is confirmed.

Seller fees

🚗

Vehicles & machinery

5% seller fee, deducted from the winning sale price. Buyers pay a 3% buyer fee.

📦

All other categories

7.5% seller fee on electronics, liquidations, repossessions, and home & business lots. Buyers pay 5%.

🔒

No reserve, no hidden costs

Every lot sells to the highest bidder. Fee terms are confirmed in writing before your auction goes live.
