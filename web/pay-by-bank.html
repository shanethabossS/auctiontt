How it works

  1. Send the amount from your online banking to the account above.
  2. Put DTT and the email address on your DealzTT account in the payment reference (for example DTT you@example.com). Also add the lot number.
  3. Email your receipt, and what you paid for, to info@sovdigitalgroup.com.
  4. We match your payment to your account and confirm by email.

Transfers from other banks can take longer to arrive. Keep your receipt until we confirm. We will never ask you to send money to a personal account — only the account name shown above.
