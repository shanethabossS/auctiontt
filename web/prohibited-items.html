Inventory safety

Only lawful, transferable, accurately described items belong on DealzTT.

This pre-launch standard is not exhaustive. DealzTT may reject or remove inventory that creates legal, safety, authenticity, privacy, or transfer risk.

Illegal or stolen property

No stolen goods, contraband, counterfeit goods, unlawful replicas, or items a seller is not authorized to transfer.

Weapons and dangerous goods

No weapons, ammunition, explosives, hazardous materials, or unsafe recalled goods without an expressly approved lawful category and process.

Controlled and regulated goods

No controlled substances, prescription products, alcohol, tobacco, wildlife products, or regulated goods without documented authorization and platform approval.

People, data, and credentials

No personal data, identity documents offered as goods, account credentials, financial access, surveillance material, or exploitative content.

Unsafe or misleading inventory

No undisclosed severe damage, odometer manipulation, hidden title problems, tampered serial numbers, or materially false images and descriptions.

Ask before submitting

If an item may be restricted, contact support before sending documents or preparing the listing.
