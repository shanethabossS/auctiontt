2. Seller commission

Sell more in a month, pay a lower rate

Commission is a percentage of the value of each completed sale. Your rate depends on how many sales you complete in the month. It does not depend on what your goods are worth or how many listings you have live.

Casual seller

8%

0 to 10 completed sales in the month.

Regular seller

7%

11 to 50 completed sales in the month.

High-volume seller

5%

51 or more completed sales in the month.

3. Buyer fee and payment costs

Buyers pay 2% of the item price

The buyer fee is separate from the seller commission, delivery costs and payment-processing charges.

Buyer fee

2% of the item sale value

Payment-processing cost

40 / 40 / 20

The real processing charge is split 40% buyer, 40% seller and 20% DealzTT. It is a share of the processor’s own fee, not an extra percentage of the price. If the processor charges TT$10, the buyer and seller each bear TT$4 and DealzTT bears TT$2.

Example, a TT$1,000 item sold by a casual seller: the seller’s commission is TT$80 and the buyer fee is TT$20. The buyer pays TT$1,020 plus any delivery and their share of processing. The seller receives TT$920 before their share of processing and your listing fee.

5. Delivery and pickup

Three ways to get the goods

  • The seller delivers.
  • The buyer collects.
  • DealzTT arranges delivery through a delivery provider. We earn 10% of the actual delivery charge, never a share of the item price. A TT$200 delivery charge means TT$20 to DealzTT.

The buyer and seller agree between themselves who pays the delivery cost.

6. Inspection, payouts and disputes

How the money is protected

Buyer inspection

Buyers have 48 hours after delivery or collection to check the item. They confirm by choosing Approved & Satisfied in the website or app.

Payment is never released automatically after 48 hours. If the buyer does not respond, our staff review the sale first.

Seller payouts

Approved sales are paid out on the next weekly (seven-day) payout cycle. A sale with an open dispute is not paid until the dispute is settled.

If something goes wrong

Buyer and seller get 72 hours to sort it out between themselves. If they cannot, DealzTT holds the seller’s payment, looks at the evidence and decides. For a defective or misrepresented item, we also decide from the evidence who pays the return shipping.

Fair enforcement

Three confirmed violations lead to a permanent seller ban. A complaint alone does not count. It must be reviewed and confirmed first.
