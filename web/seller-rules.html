Planned seller rules

Publish only items you can accurately describe and transfer.

Businesses and individuals may apply. Approval remains mandatory for every seller and every lot before an auction is scheduled.

Verify identity and ownership

Provide the required identity, business, ownership, or authority-to-sell evidence through the secure review workflow, never through public feedback.

Describe the item honestly

Disclose condition, defects, damage, missing parts, document status, location, fulfillment method, and clear current images.

No reserves

Once approved and published, the lot sells to the highest valid bidder. Sellers cannot add a hidden reserve or cancel to avoid a result.

Support viewing and handoff

Honor published viewing, pickup, and delivery commitments. Keep the item and documents available through the collection deadline.

Fees and payout

The planned success fee is 5% for vehicles and machinery and 7.5% for other categories. Payout follows confirmed pickup or delivery.

Restricted inventory

Review the prohibited-items standard before preparing a consignment.
