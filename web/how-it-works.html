How it works

Bidding on DealzTT, start to finish.

Timed auctions, no reserves, transparent fees, and a clear handoff. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you find a lot to the moment it is yours.

Four simple steps

1

Find a lot & sign in

Search or browse by category, location, price, or time left. Create a free DealzTT account with SOV ID — you must be signed in to bid, watch, or pay.

2

Place your bid

Bid the minimum increment or more. Choose manual bidding or set a proxy maximum and let DealzTT bid for you up to your limit. You are notified instantly if you're outbid.

3

Win at soft close

No reserves — the highest bid wins. Any bid in the final two minutes extends the auction by two minutes, so late snipers can't steal a lot. Highest bidder at close wins.

4

Pay & collect

Pay within 48 hours through TTPay. Collect within five business days, or arrange delivery for shipped items. Seller is paid after pickup or delivery is confirmed.

Fees & rules

Know the cost before you bid

Open fee calculator
🚗

Vehicles & machinery

3% buyer fee, 5% seller fee. Each lot shows its final fee snapshot before bidding opens — no surprises at checkout.

📦

All other categories

5% buyer fee, 7.5% seller fee for electronics, liquidations, repossessions, and home & business lots.

⏱️

Timing & soft close

Auctions default to seven days. No reserve on any lot. A two-minute soft close repeats whenever there's a last-second bid.

Buying & selling with confidence

✅

Verified sellers

Seller labels, verification status, and location appear on every lot. Buyer rules cover bids, inspection, and payment.

🚩

Report any lot

One-tap reporting on every listing routes straight to the DealzTT team. Seller rules cover ownership, disclosure, and fulfillment.

Safety & support
