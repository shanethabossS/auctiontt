Before you participate

Clear information before any bid.

DealzTT is preparing its first catalogue of vehicles, machinery, repossessions, liquidations, and general goods. Only lots published on the marketplace are available to review.

1. Review the lot record

Check the title, current price, end time, seller details, location, images, and condition information published for that specific lot. Ask for clarification through the feedback portal when a material detail is missing.

2. Understand the total cost

DealzTT has not published a final buyer or seller fee schedule. A fee, payment deadline, and collection terms must be shown for the specific auction before bidding is enabled.

3. Bid only when ready

The current site does not publish proxy bidding, reserve status, deposits, automatic extensions, or winner/payment records. Do not assume any of those protections exist until they are stated on the live lot.

4. Use trust signals carefully

A seller label is not a substitute for a published condition record or ownership documentation. Vehicle inspections, pickup scheduling, and dispute handling are not available on the current public site.

For consignors

Vehicles, machinery, repossessions, and liquidation inventory need review before publication. Use the seller hub to prepare a consignment request; publication and payout workflows are not live yet.
