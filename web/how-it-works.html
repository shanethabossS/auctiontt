Before you participate

Clear information before any bid.

DealzTT is preparing auctions across all six marketplace categories. Only lots published on the marketplace are available to review.

1. Review the lot record

Check the price, closing time, seller, location, evidence, condition, fees, and fulfillment method shown for that specific lot.

2. Preview the total cost

The planned buyer fee is 3% for vehicles and machinery and 5% for other categories. The lot must show its final fee snapshot before bidding opens.

Open fee calculator

3. Understand the auction mode

Each auction declares manual or proxy bidding. There are no reserves, and a bid in the final two minutes repeats a two-minute soft close.

Read auction rules

4. Complete payment and handoff

The planned full TTPay payment is due within 48 hours. Pickup is due within five business days, or seller payout follows confirmed delivery for shipped items. These flows are not live yet.

Buyer and seller responsibilities

Buyer rules cover bids, inspection, and payment. Seller rules cover ownership, disclosure, and fulfillment.

Safety and support

Review prohibited items, privacy, and the current terms notice. Report concerns through support.
