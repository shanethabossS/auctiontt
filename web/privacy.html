Current pre-launch notice

Privacy and safe information sharing.

This page describes the current public preview. Final transaction, identity-document, payment, retention, and rights disclosures require legal approval before auctions launch.

Public browsing

You can browse public categories and published lots without submitting personal information.

Accounts and feedback

Account and feedback services may process the information you submit to provide access, respond to requests, prevent abuse, and maintain service records.

Do not send sensitive data publicly

Never place passwords, payment credentials, one-time codes, unredacted identity documents, or full ownership-document numbers in feedback.

Future transaction records

Bids, payment, verification, messages, and handoff evidence are not active DealzTT workflows today. Their access and retention rules must be published before activation.
